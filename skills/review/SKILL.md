---
name: review
description: "Check the Afterchat vault's unchecked drafts and Tidy up's suggestions against the code. Use when someone asks to review, verify or clean up knowledge base drafts."
---

Go through the draft entries, the answers nobody has checked yet: list_entries with state "draft", every page, and open each with entry_id. Check each against the code. Fix what is wrong or missing with propose_entry (update_entry_id, the files in code_refs). Where two drafts say the same, keep the better one up to date and list the other. Then list_suggestions: settle each disagreement and update from a chat against the code with tidy, saying why with the files, and apply the organizing ones that make sense. Only a person can mark an entry checked, so finish with a short list: the drafts that look right to verify, the duplicates, the picks left in Tidy up, and anything you could not settle.
